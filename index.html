"Tiffin service for office-goers, home kitchen, ₹30,000 capital, Kolhapur"

GO — WITH CHANGES

Solid demand in a tier-2 office market, but ₹30,000 won't cover a second cook past month one — start solo and reinvest before hiring.

Why

Office tiffin demand in Kolhapur is steady and repeat-order-driven, and home-kitchen models keep fixed costs near zero. The risk isn't demand — it's whether ₹30,000 stretches through the slow first 3–4 weeks before word-of-mouth kicks in.

Watch out for

  • FSSAI registration and basic kitchen hygiene compliance before first sale
  • Delivery cost eating margin if you use aggregators instead of a fixed local route
  • Menu fatigue — repeat customers churn fastest when the menu doesn't rotate

Do this first

  • Pre-sell 15 tiffins to neighbours/coworkers before spending on packaging
  • Register on FSSAI's basic registration (not license) — it's cheap and fast at this scale
  • Fix a 6-day rotating menu now, so costing is predictable from day one
