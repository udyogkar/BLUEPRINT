Is the free verdict really free?

Yes. The initial business verdict is designed to give you a useful decision before asking for payment.

Is this a generic AI business plan?

No. The product is designed around your idea, budget, location, operating model and goals. The paid reports are structured around practical execution rather than generic motivational content.

Will the blueprint guarantee profit?

No. A blueprint can improve decisions and reduce avoidable mistakes; it cannot guarantee demand, revenue or profit.

Yes. Select the blueprint language before generating your verdict. Initial launch languages are English, Hindi and Marathi.
